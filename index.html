    Who Farted?

    A comedy deduction game. Someone farted at the worst possible moment. You work out who, and present the evidence in front of everyone, without anyone deciding it was you.

    Investigate

    The fart is a live gas simulation that rides the room's air currents. What someone ate decides how loud it was and how it smells, based on real gut science. Everyone tells the truth except the culprit. Overhear whispers, snoop bags, find the photo.

    Keep your aura

    Your reputation is the fail state. Everyone has a line of sight, some notice more than others, suspicion spreads as gossip, and playground law applies: smelt it, dealt it. You also need to go, so you are a suspect too.

    Prove it

    Everyone forms a theory, and you can plant rumours to steer the room. When it is time to call it, the accused testifies and you break each statement with evidence, Ace Attorney style.

    Why this, why now

    Fart games exist: Bad Air Day on iOS, the board game PRRRT..., and a multiplayer hidden-role game called Who Fart? listed on Steam. None of them is a detective game. Comedy deduction sells: the Frog Detective series has sold over 500,000 copies, and reviewers of Duck Detective: The Secret Salami mainly complained that it was too short.
